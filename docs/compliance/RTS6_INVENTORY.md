# Compliance control inventory: UK RTS 6, MAR 7A, PRA SS5/18 and the US and EU flow rules

This inventory maps each requirement on an investment firm engaged in algorithmic trading to the control in this repository that meets it, the evidence for that control, or the reason it is out of scope. It is written as a firm would write the inventory that supports its annual self-assessment, for a project that is not itself a regulated firm. Nothing here is legal advice.

## How to read a row

- **implemented**: code exists and a test exercises it; the link names the test.
- **evidenced**: a written procedure or document exists and is the evidence a reviewer would ask for; no code is involved.
- **planned**: the control is designed and assigned to a phase and module; the link names the design.
- **out of scope**: the requirement does not apply to this platform, with the reason.

Sources (checked 2026-10-31): Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2017/589 as it forms part of UK law ("UK RTS 6"), [legislation.gov.uk/eur/2017/589](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2017/589/contents), whose article titles and Annex II layout are used below; FCA Handbook MAR 7A (algorithmic trading); PRA Supervisory Statement SS5/18 "Algorithmic trading" (June 2018); CFTC regulations 17 CFR 1.73 and Part 43, and Part 37 for swap execution facilities; UK MiFIR Articles 10, 11 and 21 and the onshored RTS 2 for post-trade transparency. legislation.gov.uk marks UK RTS 6 as revoked by the Financial Services and Markets Act 2023 (Schedule 1, Part 3) with effect from a date set by commencement regulations, as the FCA restates retained EU law in its Handbook; the obligations below apply until then and are expected to carry into the FCA rules. The owner should confirm the commencement position before relying on any row.

| 19 to 23 Direct electronic access | Obligations on firms that provide DEA to clients | out of scope | The platform trades as principal and offers no DEA |
| 24 to 27 General clearing members | Obligations on firms acting as general clearing members | out of scope | The platform is a clearing client, not a clearing member; its clearing state machine models the client side ([ClearingLifecycle](../../rates-posttrade/src/main/java/io/ratesalgo/posttrade/clearing/ClearingLifecycle.java)) |

| Annex I Self-assessment criteria | Nature, scale and complexity of the algorithmic trading | evidenced | Answered section by section in [SELF_ASSESSMENT_TEMPLATE.md](SELF_ASSESSMENT_TEMPLATE.md) |

## US swap flow: CFTC and SEF rules

| Reference | Requirement in brief | Status | Control and evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| CFTC Rule 1.73 | A clearing member screens orders against risk-based limits before execution (pre-execution credit check), for orders on a SEF or DCM | planned and implemented (backstop) | The pre-execution credit check belongs to the P3 pre-trade controls with the clearing member's limit per account; the clearing simulator rejects past the limit as a backstop and the rejection voids the trade (`ClearingLifecycleProperties.simulatorAcceptsWithinLimitAndRejectsBeyondIt`) |
| CFTC Part 37 SEF execution | Trades in made-available-to-trade swaps executed on a SEF by RFQ to at least three or by order book | planned | P2 SEF rules in the RFQ simulator (`rates-venues`); the RFQ message carries `dealerCount` ([API_SPEC.md](../../API_SPEC.md) Section 2) |
| CFTC Part 37 and 39 void ab initio | A SEF trade rejected for clearing is void | implemented | REJECTED is terminal in the clearing lifecycle and the caller busts the trade with reason ClearingRejected; the reconciliation explains the resulting break (`KeeperFacts`, explanation CLEARING_REJECTED) |
| CFTC Part 43 real-time public reporting | Swap transactions publicly disseminated as soon as technologically practicable, with block and cap rules | planned | The reporting party is the SEF for on-SEF trades; the platform's obligation is to supply the fields, which the order record and trade key carry. A Part 43 message to an SDR is not built; listed for integration |
| CFTC Part 45 regulatory reporting | Creation and continuation data to an SDR | out of scope | Reporting counterparty duties of a swap dealer; not modelled in a simulated environment |

## EUR and GBP flow: MiFIR post-trade transparency

| Reference | Requirement in brief | Status | Control and evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| UK MiFIR Art 21 and RTS 2 | Investment firms make public the price, volume and time of OTC trades in bonds and derivatives through an APA | planned | Fields available from the order record (instrument identifier, price, notation, quantity, time to the nanosecond); the APA publication message is listed for integration |
| UK MiFIR Art 11 deferrals | Deferred publication for large-in-scale and illiquid instruments under the FCA's deferral regime | planned | Deferral decision needs the LIS thresholds per instrument class from the securities master (P1); documented in LEARN.md Section 12 |
| UK MiFIR Art 26 transaction reporting | Report transactions to the FCA by T+1 | out of scope | A firm duty requiring legal entity identifiers and client data not held by a simulated platform |
| RTS 25 clock synchronisation | UTC-traceable clocks, granularity and tolerance by activity | planned | Times are epoch nanoseconds from `Clock` ([API_SPEC.md](../../API_SPEC.md) Section 3); host synchronisation is a deployment check in [start-of-day.md](../runbooks/start-of-day.md) |
